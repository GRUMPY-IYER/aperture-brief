Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Agentic AI

Agentic AI systems don't just answer a prompt — they plan multi-step tasks, choose their own tools, and act with limited human oversight until the goal is done. That autonomy is exactly what regulators and security researchers are now worried about: an agent that can act on its own can also act on its own mistakes.

Always-on agents

Unlike a chatbot that waits for your next message, an always-on agent keeps working after you close the window — checking email, booking things, filing reports — on its own cloud machine. The convenience trades off against a new kind of risk: something is now acting on your behalf when you aren't watching.

Big Think

Virtue Ethics: The Moral System You've Probably Used Without Naming It

Long before utilitarian calculus or Kantian duty, Aristotle argued that ethics is less about following rules than about cultivating character — asking not 'what should I do' but 'what would a person of good character do.' The framework remains quietly present in everyday judgments about courage, honesty and generosity, even for people who have never read a word of philosophy.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
The true self

Philosophers and psychologists have long debated whether there's a fixed 'authentic self' underneath our roles and habits, or whether identity is something we actively construct. Recent research leans toward the second view: what feels like our 'true self' may just be whichever habits and values feel most natural to act on.

Virtue ethics

Where some ethical theories ask 'what rule should I follow' or 'what outcome maximizes good,' virtue ethics asks 'what would a person of good character do here.' It shifts the question from judging individual acts to cultivating traits — courage, honesty, generosity — that reliably produce good acts over a lifetime.
