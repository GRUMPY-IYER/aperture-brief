Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
"Surveillance as a service"

A business model where a private company (like license-plate-reader maker Flock) sells cities and police departments access to a surveillance network — cameras, data storage, analytics — as an ongoing subscription rather than a one-time purchase. It's why so many small-town city councils, like Norman's in today's story, end up wrestling with data-retention and privacy questions usually associated with much bigger tech companies.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Idealism (in the New Thought sense, above)

The broad philosophical position that mind or consciousness is more fundamental than physical matter — not just one modern theory, but a family of views with a long, varied history, including the 19th-century New Thought movement discussed above, which is a very different tradition from analytic idealism but shares the basic premise that mind comes first.

Psychotechnology

A term for culturally developed practices or tools — meditation, writing, the Socratic method, ritual — that reshape how the mind processes and makes sense of experience. The idea is that changing how you think isn't just about having new facts, but about adopting new "technologies" for attention and meaning-making.
