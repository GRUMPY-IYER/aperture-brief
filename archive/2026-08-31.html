Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Export controls

Rules restricting the sale or transfer of certain goods and technology to specific countries, usually for national-security reasons. They rarely stop trade outright — more often they redirect it toward whichever markets a restricted country can still sell into.

Teleoperation

Remotely piloting a vehicle or robot from a distance rather than fully automated control. Many "self-driving" fleets still lean on remote human backup for tricky moments — which is where a lot of the hidden labor sits.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Thucydides Trap

The idea that war becomes likely when a rising power threatens to displace an established one, each side's fear feeding the other's. The term comes from Thucydides' history, though today's essay argues his real point was subtler than that.

Epistemic colonialism

When a dominant intellectual tradition absorbs ideas from outside it only by translating them into its own terms — technically "included," but stripped of whatever made them a genuine challenge in the first place.
