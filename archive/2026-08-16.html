Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
AI Risk Report / Threat Model

When AI companies build a powerful new model, they write a report grading how likely it is to cause different kinds of harm — similar to a safety inspection before shipping a product. A "threat model" is just one category of risk being watched (e.g. "could this help build a weapon?" vs. "could it mess with a company's own systems?"). Labs update these ratings as new evidence comes in, the way a weather forecast updates as a storm develops.

Run rate

A company's most recent month or quarter of revenue multiplied out to a full year — used to describe fast-growing companies before their "real" annual numbers exist. Useful for making sense of headlines about multi-billion-dollar quarters.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Panpsychism

The view that consciousness, or some proto-conscious quality, is a fundamental and ubiquitous feature of matter itself — not something that only emerges in brains. Instead of asking "how does dead matter produce experience?", panpsychists ask "how do simple bits of experience combine into a mind like yours?" — a swap of one hard puzzle for another, as today's item argues.
