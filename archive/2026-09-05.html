Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Self-certification

Under US auto safety rules, a manufacturer can either seek a specific regulatory exemption for a novel vehicle design or self-certify that it already meets existing standards. Tesla chose the second path for Cybercab's lack of a steering wheel — faster to market, but it puts the company's own compliance judgment on the line if regulators disagree.

Advertising ID

A unique, resettable identifier every phone assigns for ad targeting, distinct from a name or phone number but valuable enough on its own — combined with location pings — for a data broker to reconstruct exactly where a specific device, and its owner, has been.

Phys.org

Five-metal 2D catalyst converts CO2 to CO without an extra jolt of electricity

Washington University researchers combined five metals — molybdenum, tungsten, vanadium, niobium and tantalum — into a 2D high-entropy MBene catalyst that converts CO2 into carbon monoxide, a building block for synthetic fuels, without the extra electrical boost most prior catalysts required. CO can then be combined with hydrogen to make methanol, recycling captured CO2 into usable fuel.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Ziran (自然)

The Daoist idea of 'self-so-ness': that action arises naturally from a convergence of circumstances rather than from an isolated will choosing to act. It underlies Zhuangzi's suspicion of meritocracy, which credits success entirely to individual choice.

Pyrrhonism vs. Academic Skepticism

Two ancient skeptical schools: Pyrrhonism suspends judgment on everything to reach calm, while Academic Skepticism holds that no belief is fully certain but some are more probable than others — letting you act while still doubting.
