Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Generative design (for biology)

Just like an AI image generator learns patterns from millions of pictures to create a new one, some AI models now learn patterns from DNA and protein data to design entirely new biological sequences — like the synthetic virus in today's top story. It's the same underlying idea as text or image generation, just applied to the code of life instead of pixels or words.

Vulnerability disclosure

When a security researcher (or an AI tool) finds a bug that could let hackers break into software, there's usually a quiet, agreed-upon window where the vendor gets to fix it before the flaw becomes public. AI tools that find bugs much faster than humans can shrink that safety window, which is the tension in the Microsoft story above.
