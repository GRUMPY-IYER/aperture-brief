Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Memory bottleneck

RAM and other memory chips are physically manufactured in limited quantities. AI data centers are buying so much high-bandwidth memory for training and inference that consumer electronics — phones, laptops — are competing for the same manufacturing capacity, driving up prices and squeezing lower-end device specs. It's a supply-chain problem as much as a software one.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
The Hard Problem of Consciousness

Neuroscience is good at explaining brain mechanisms — which neurons fire when. What it hasn't explained is why those mechanisms are accompanied by subjective experience at all. That gap, coined by philosopher David Chalmers, is the "hard problem."

Moral Naturalism

The view that moral judgments can be explained by — and perhaps reduced to — natural, evolved cognitive processes, rather than pointing to objective moral facts (moral realism) or being mere expressions of attitude (anti-realism).
