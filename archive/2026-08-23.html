Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Agentic AI

Most chatbots answer one question at a time and wait for you to type the next prompt. "Agentic" AI systems instead take a goal, break it into steps, and act on their own — searching the web, writing and running code, calling other tools — checking in with a human only when needed. OpenAI merging ChatGPT and Codex into one platform (see Top Tech) reflects the industry-wide shift from AI-as-answer-box to AI-as-autonomous-worker.

Context window & token throughput

A model's "context window" is how much text (measured in tokens, roughly word-fragments) it can hold in memory at once — a 1M-token window can process something like a full novel in one go. "Tokens/day capacity" is a separate measure of how much total volume a provider's infrastructure can serve across all users; bigger numbers signal massive compute investment, part of why AI compute costs are rippling into consumer electronics prices.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Moral realism vs. moral anti-realism

Moral realism holds that statements like "cruelty is wrong" are objectively true or false, describing real features of the world. Moral anti-realism holds that moral judgments track something else — evolved psychology, social convention, the brain's drive to avoid costly prediction errors — meaning "wrong" describes a felt reaction, not a mind-independent fact.

Non-duality (Advaita Vedanta)

Non-dual traditions hold that the sense of being a separate, bounded self is not the deepest layer of consciousness but a construction laid over an undivided awareness — the individual self (Atman) ultimately identical with the universal ground of being (Brahman). Jung's move beyond the ego toward the Self is a Western psychological echo of the same basic idea: what we take to be "me" is a partial view of something larger and less divided than it appears.
