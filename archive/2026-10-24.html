Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Foundation model

A foundation model is a large AI system trained once on broad data, then adapted — via smaller follow-up training or prompts — to many specific tasks, rather than built from scratch for each one.

Pre-money valuation

Pre-money valuation is what a company is said to be worth right before new investment money comes in; add the new cash to get the 'post-money' figure investors actually own a slice of.

IAI News

Moral Responsibility Without Free Will

Galen Strawson argues free will is impossible under either determinism or indeterminism, yet insists we cannot simply drop our everyday sense that people are responsible for what they do. The essay sits with that contradiction rather than resolving it, treating it as a basic fact about human life.

IAI News

Free Will Is an Invention

Alexandre Fillon revisits Nietzsche's case that free will is a fiction invented to make guilt and punishment feel justified, not a fact about how choices actually happen. Dropping the fiction, Fillon argues, doesn't flatten moral life — it relocates it onto character and consequence instead of blame.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Hard determinism

Hard determinism is the view that every event, including every human choice, is the inevitable result of prior causes — so free will, in the sense of genuinely being able to have done otherwise, doesn't exist.

Compatibilism

Compatibilism tries to keep moral responsibility even if determinism is true, by redefining 'free' to mean acting from your own reasons and desires without external coercion — not escaping causation altogether.
