Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Training data provenance

This just means knowing where the material used to train an AI model actually came from — which books, images or web pages — and whether whoever owns that material agreed to let it be used. Companies are under growing pressure to be able to answer that question honestly.

Text watermarking

A way of hiding a detectable pattern inside AI-generated text — invisible to a normal reader, but identifiable by a special tool — so that the text can later be traced back to the AI model that wrote it.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Existentialism

The idea, associated with Sartre, that there's no fixed blueprint for what a person is supposed to be — we exist first, then define ourselves through our choices, and that freedom is both exhilarating and a little terrifying.

The hard problem of consciousness

Scientists can map which brain patterns go with which mental states, but that doesn't explain why any of it is accompanied by subjective experience at all — why there's "something it's like" to be you rather than just a machine processing signals in the dark.
