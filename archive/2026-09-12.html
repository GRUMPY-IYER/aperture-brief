Anthropic says its models can no longer be assumed safe from helping build bioweapons

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Moral relativism vs. moral absolutism

Absolutism holds that some acts are right or wrong regardless of context; relativism holds that judgment depends on circumstance, intention and culture. Many real-world disagreements are really about which of these two lenses to use.

Personal identity

The philosophical question of what makes you the same person over time, given that your body, memories and beliefs all change — do you persist because of a continuous body, a continuous mind, or something else entirely?
