TechCrunch

Microsoft 365 outage drags into a second day

A Microsoft 365 outage that began in Exchange Online on August 31 spread to Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Purview and Defender XDR, disrupting mail and collaboration tools for a second straight day. Microsoft traced it to a core authentication configuration issue and said recovery was underway.

Why it matters for you: A single authentication misconfiguration cascaded across six separate Microsoft 365 services at once — a reminder that in a consolidated SaaS stack, auth is the one component whose blast radius touches everything built on top of it.

Axios

FTC and 22 states sue Amazon over hidden ad-auction surcharges

The FTC and 22 state attorneys general sued Amazon, alleging it secretly overcharged advertisers more than $20 billion since 2019 by layering a hidden surcharge on top of its stated second-price ad auction. The suit says roughly 1.2 million advertisers, nearly half of them small businesses, paid more than they were told.

Why it matters for you: If your product documents how a price or ranking is calculated, that description becomes a legal claim — this case turns entirely on the gap between the auction mechanism Amazon published and the one it actually ran.

Techmeme

EU designates ChatGPT a 'Very Large Online Search Engine'

The European Commission designated ChatGPT a 'Very Large Online Search Engine' under the Digital Services Act — the first such label for a standalone AI chatbot — after OpenAI reported more than 159 million monthly EU users, well past the 45-million threshold. Why it matters for you: Crossing a monthly-active-user number, not shipping any particular feature, is what triggered a new compliance regime here — map your own product's usage thresholds against regulatory tripwires before growth outruns legal review.

Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Second-price auction

In a second-price auction, the highest bidder wins but pays only what the second-highest bidder offered — the standard model for online ad auctions because it's meant to make bidders bid their true value rather than game the price. The FTC's case against Amazon alleges it quietly moved away from this rule while still describing its auction as second-price.

Circular financing

Circular financing is when a company invests in or extends credit to a customer who then spends that money buying the investor's own product, inflating both sides' apparent growth without new outside cash entering the system. In the Anthropic-Lambda deal, Nvidia is simultaneously Lambda's backer, landlord and chip supplier, all ultimately funded by payments flowing back to Nvidia.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Teleology

The study of purpose or goal-directedness in nature — asking not just how something works but what it's for. Biologists have long been wary of the word for sounding like it implies conscious design, even while using purpose-talk (a heart 'is for' pumping blood) constantly in practice.

The banality of evil

Hannah Arendt's argument, from her reporting on Adolf Eichmann's trial, that atrocity is often committed not by monsters but by ordinary people failing to think critically about what they are doing. It's a caution that unexamined obedience, not dramatic villainy, is what makes cruelty possible at scale.
