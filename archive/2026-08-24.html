Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Sole-source contract

A government contract awarded to one vendor without a competitive bidding process, usually because officials argue only that vendor can do the job (or do it fast enough). Critics say it's also an easy way to avoid scrutiny — like ICE's LexisNexis deal above.

Open-weight model

An AI model whose trained parameters ("weights") are published for anyone to download and run on their own hardware, as opposed to a "closed" model you can only access through a company's paid API. Open-weight doesn't necessarily mean open-source — the training code and data are often still secret.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Idealism (philosophical)

The view that reality is fundamentally mental or experiential rather than physical — that consciousness comes first, and matter is something consciousness gives rise to, not the other way around. It's the inverse of materialism, which says mind emerges from matter.

Gödel's incompleteness theorem

A result in mathematical logic showing that in any sufficiently complex formal system, there will always be true statements that can't be proven using only the rules of that system. Often read more broadly as a limit on how completely any single framework — logical, scientific, or otherwise — can capture the whole truth.
