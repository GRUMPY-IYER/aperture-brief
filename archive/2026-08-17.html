Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
AI Gateway

A middleman service that lets a company plug into many different AI models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, and others) through one connection, then switch between them for cost or performance without rewriting their product. Today's Stripe/OpenRouter deal is built around this idea.

Vertical Integration

When a company buys or builds the pieces upstream of its product — like SpaceX owning both the AI coding tool Cursor and the data centers/GPUs that power it — instead of relying on outside suppliers.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Generic Generalization

A belief like "cats are furry" that describes what's normally true of a category without claiming it's true of every single member — unlike "all cats are furry," it doesn't get thrown out the moment you meet one hairless cat.

Cosmic Bounce

An alternative to the Big Bang where, instead of everything starting from a single explosive point, the Universe was previously a different universe that contracted down to a very dense state and then "bounced" back outward.
