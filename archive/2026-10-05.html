AI companies face a wave of court fights built on their own public safety warnings and a trail of training-data evidence, Axios reports. Why it matters for you: Legal exposure for what your AI feature outputs is becoming a product-design question, not just a legal one — know your company's liability theory before you ship.

Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Section 230

The 1996 law shielding internet platforms from liability for content users post. Courts are increasingly treating AI chatbot outputs as the platform's own speech rather than a user's, so this shield may not apply.

AI slop

Low-quality, often hallucinated content mass-produced by AI and submitted as if genuine — now overwhelming systems built for human-scale volume, from bug bounty programs to search results.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Instrumentalism

The view that a scientific theory's job is to predict and organize observations correctly, not to describe what's 'really' out there — contrasted with realism, which takes the theory's entities at face value.

Parity

Philosopher Ruth Chang's term for options that are neither better, worse, nor exactly equal — genuinely hard to compare, which is why some choices resist being settled by more information.
