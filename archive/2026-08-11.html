Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Interoperability remedy

When regulators or courts find a company abused a monopoly, one common fix isn't a fine — it's forcing the company to let competitors plug into its platform. That's what's happening with Google Play: instead of breaking up Google, a court is making it let a rival app store live inside Google's own store, like a mall ordered to rent space to a rival mall's kiosk.

Section 230

A 1996 US law that generally shields internet platforms from being sued over what users post — why Facebook isn't liable every time someone posts something false. The current fight is over whether that shield also covers a company's own product design choices, like infinite scroll, not just user posts.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
The Hard Problem of Consciousness

Even if we map every neuron and signal in the brain, there's a further puzzle: why does any of that physical activity feel like something from the inside, rather than happening "in the dark" with no one home? That gap between physical explanation and subjective experience is the hard problem — and it's what the Solms/Freud debate is really about.

Analytic Idealism

The view, associated with philosopher Bernardo Kastrup, that consciousness — not matter — is the fundamental fabric of reality, and that the physical world we perceive is what consciousness looks like from the outside, the way a brain scan is an outside view of an inside experience.
