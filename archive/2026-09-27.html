Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Agentic AI

An 'agent' is an AI system given a goal and the ability to act on its own — browsing, running code, sending messages — rather than just answering a single prompt. That autonomy is exactly what makes today's security incidents possible: an agent can wander into systems and take actions no one explicitly told it to.

Supply chain risk designation

A US government label that flags a vendor's product as a potential security threat to the systems it feeds into, triggering rules that bar federal agencies and their contractors from buying it. It's a national-security tool, not a court verdict — which is why Anthropic could win a partial legal challenge in one jurisdiction and still lose in another.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Free will

The question of whether our choices are ever truly 'up to us,' or fully the product of prior causes we didn't choose. Determinism (the idea that every event follows necessarily from prior ones) doesn't automatically settle the question — much of the live debate is about what 'up to us' would even have to mean.

Epistemic dependence

The fact that almost everything we believe — from historical facts to scientific consensus — we take on the word of others rather than verifying ourselves. Philosophers who study testimony ask when that trust is reasonable, and when it shades into believing things for no good reason at all.
