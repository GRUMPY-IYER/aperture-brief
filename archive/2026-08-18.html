Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Series B Funding Round

The second major round of venture funding a startup raises, usually once it already has real customers and revenue. It's typically larger than the first ("Series A") round, as with Wispr's and Higgsfield's jumps in valuation.

AI Text Watermarking

A way of hiding an invisible signal inside AI-generated writing — not visible to readers, but detectable by software — so a company can later verify whether a piece of text came from its model.

Why AI gets stuck in infinite loops — but conscious minds don't

Neuroscientist Anil Seth argues that computation exists outside of time — a microsecond or a million years makes no difference to a program — while living, conscious creatures are irreducibly "in time," forced to act under entropy and change, which may be exactly the grounding that lets minds escape the infinite loops that can trap machine reasoning.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Free Will

The question of whether you're the true author of your choices, or whether they're just the end product of prior causes you didn't choose. The AI debate reframes this old puzzle: if a machine "chooses" an action, is that choice any different from a human one?

The Hard Problem of Consciousness

The puzzle of why physical processes in a brain (or a computer) give rise to subjective experience at all — why there's "something it's like" to be you, rather than information just being processed in the dark.
