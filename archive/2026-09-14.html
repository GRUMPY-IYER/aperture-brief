Asian AI Stocks Slide as Anthropic, OpenAI and Musk Call for Slower Development

SoftBank fell as much as 13% in Tokyo and chipmakers Kioxia, SK Hynix and Samsung all dropped sharply after Anthropic's Dario Amodei, OpenAI's Sam Altman and Elon Musk each backed calls over the weekend to deliberately pace frontier AI development. Altman Says OpenAI Won't Wait for Legislation to Pace AI Development

Sam Altman said OpenAI "welcomes a federal framework" for AI safety but does not believe the industry needs to wait for legislation or an antitrust exemption to start building confidence with outside auditors. Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Pacing the frontier

A voluntary, industry-wide slowdown in how fast AI labs release more capable models — as opposed to a government-imposed pause — meant to leave more time for safety work without any one company falling behind.

Adjusted operating income

A profitability measure that strips out one-time or non-cash costs to show whether a business's core operations are actually in the black — the number Anthropic is highlighting to IPO investors instead of raw revenue growth.

ScienceDaily

Global Warming Is Breaking a 400-Year Climate Link Between Two Oceans

Paleoclimate records show the Indian and Pacific Oceans' atmospheric circulations stayed coupled for most of the last 400 years, interrupted only briefly by 19th-century volcanic eruptions. Woods Hole researchers found that human-caused warming has now driven an exceptional, sustained breakdown in that coupling since the 1980s, with no precedent in the historical record.

Aeon

Four Islamic Polymaths Prove Breadth Beats Specialisation

Aeon profiles al-Biruni, Alhazen, Avicenna and al-Khwarizmi, four scholars who moved freely between mathematics, medicine, astronomy and philosophy despite living under wildly different circumstances, from house arrest to court patronage. Their shared habit of testing one discipline's assumptions against another's, the essay argues, produced breakthroughs that narrower specialists of their eras could not have reached alone.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Confucian meritocracy

A political model that selects some or all leaders by demonstrated ability and virtue rather than by popular vote, often proposed as a complement to or replacement for one-person-one-vote democracy.

Polymathy

The practice of working seriously across multiple, seemingly unrelated fields rather than specializing in one — a habit several of history's most original thinkers shared, and one modern academic specialization tends to discourage.
