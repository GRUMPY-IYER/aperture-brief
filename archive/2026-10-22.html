Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Model Context Protocol (MCP)

A common language that lets an AI agent connect to a company's systems — like Walmart's store inventory — without a custom integration for every retailer. Think of it as a standard plug shape instead of a different adapter for every wall socket.

Non-voting stock

Shares that pay out like regular stock but carry no vote on company decisions. Issuing them lets a company raise money from investors without handing over any control — Meta's founder keeps the same say over the company either way.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Principle of Sufficient Reason

The idea, traced to Leibniz, that everything which exists or happens has a reason or cause — nothing is just a brute, unexplained fact. Critics, including Hume, questioned whether that's actually true of the universe.

Epistemic intuition

A gut sense that something is true or false, arrived at without working through an argument. Philosophers debate whether that sense is just a feeling, or whether — like seeing a tree — it can genuinely justify a belief.
