Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
LPU / Inference Accelerator

GPUs are great at massive parallel computation — that's what makes them good for training AI models. LPUs (or "inference chips") are built instead for fast, low-latency word-by-word generation — basically the "typing speed" of an AI agent. That's why data centers now stock two different kinds of AI chips instead of one.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

As people increasingly ask chatbots questions instead of searching Google, companies and governments have started writing content specifically for AI crawlers — using tools like llms.txt — to influence what a chatbot says about them. Same instinct as SEO, just aimed at machines instead of humans.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
The Hard Problem of Consciousness

Even after scientists map every neuron involved in seeing the color red, there's still a leftover question: why does it feel like anything at all to see red? That gap between the physical description and the subjective experience is what philosophers call the "hard problem."

Non-duality (Advaita)

The idea that the innermost witness of your experience — the "you" that's aware — isn't a personal possession that gets battered by circumstance. In Advaita Vedanta, that awareness (Atman) is identical with the unconditioned ground of all reality (Brahman), which is why suffering, rightly understood, can't touch what you most fundamentally are.
