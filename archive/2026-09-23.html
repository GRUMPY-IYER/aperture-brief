TechCrunch

Hacking Group Claims It Breached the FBI, Stole Employee Data

ShinyHunters says it exploited a zero-day in Oracle's PeopleSoft software to breach FBI systems Monday night, stealing 2-3TB of data on current and former employees, job applicants and their families before defacing the FBI's jobs site. The FBI confirmed it is investigating; the group says the attack is retaliation for a May FBI report on its tactics.

Why it matters for you: Enterprise HR software is a soft underbelly most security reviews skip — a single unpatched vendor zero-day can expose an entire workforce, contractors included.

Microsoft is laying off 268 more Xbox employees and folding several studios into Activision, which will now lead development of the next Halo game and oversee Rare and Age of Empires maker World's Edge. It's the latest round in a restructuring that aims to cut roughly a fifth of Xbox's workforce by mid-2027.

Why it matters for you: Folding acquired studios into the biggest earner rather than running them independently is a pattern worth recognizing — post-merger integration usually arrives as a second, quieter wave of cuts.

Techmeme

AI Glasses Shipments Surged 263% in the First Half of 2026

Global AI glasses shipments rose 263% year-over-year in H1 2026, per Counterpoint Research, with Meta's Ray-Ban partnership driving 94% of the display-less segment that now makes up 96% of the category. EssilorLuxottica says the two companies have already sold more than 7 million pairs, with 10 million a realistic target by year's end.

Why it matters for you: The category winning is the cheap, display-less glasses, not the flashier AR headset — a reminder that adoption curves usually reward the boring, affordable version first.

Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Zero-day exploit

A vulnerability in software that the vendor doesn't yet know about — and so hasn't patched — meaning attackers can use it before any fix exists, as ShinyHunters says it did against Oracle's PeopleSoft.

Display-less AI glasses

Smart glasses that skip the built-in screen, using only a camera, speaker and microphone to let an AI assistant see and hear what you do — cheaper and lighter than AR glasses, which is why they're outselling them.

A Key Atlantic Ocean Current Has Been Weakening for Two Decades, Data Shows

University of Miami researchers analyzing two decades of data from four ocean-monitoring arrays found some of the strongest direct observational evidence yet that the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation is losing strength. We Don't Have Different Perspectives, We Inhabit Different Realities

Sociologist Steve Fuller argues that treating disagreement as a matter of differing 'perspectives' on one shared world understates how differently people actually construct reality itself. Debating Wikipedia founder Jimmy Wales and social theorist Sally Haslanger at a London festival, Fuller pushed a harder claim: that epistemic relativism runs deeper than perspective ever captures.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Philosophical scepticism

The position that we should withhold firm belief where evidence is genuinely inconclusive — not doubting everything as a stunt, but practicing it as an ongoing discipline against overconfidence.

Epistemic relativism

The idea that what counts as true or real can differ not just in opinion but in the very framework a person or culture uses to construct reality — a stronger claim than simply having a 'different perspective.'
