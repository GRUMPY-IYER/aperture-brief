Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
What's an S-1 (IPO prospectus)?

Before a company can sell shares to the public, it has to file a document called an S-1 with US regulators. It discloses financials, ownership — and, crucially, a "risk factors" section where the company has to admit, in writing, everything that could go wrong with its business. That's why it's notable when a company lists something like "public backlash" as a risk: it's a legal admission, not just a headline.

AI protein design ("minibinders")

A minibinder is a small, custom-designed protein built to latch onto a specific target in the body — the first step in making many drugs. Normally that takes human chemists weeks of trial and error. Having an AI model propose designs that a real lab can then build and test is a bit like using a chatbot to draft blueprints that engineers then actually construct and stress-test.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Non-duality

Non-dual traditions like Advaita Vedanta hold that the apparent separateness of things — self versus world, one nation versus another — is a surface appearance over a deeper, undivided reality. It's the philosophical idea behind today's Noema piece: that "us versus them" thinking in politics may be less a fact about the world than a habit of perception.

The hard problem of consciousness

Neuroscience can map which brain regions light up when you see red. It's much harder to explain why any of that activity is accompanied by an inner experience at all, rather than happening "in the dark," with no one home. That gap — between explaining the mechanism and explaining the felt experience — is what philosopher David Chalmers called the hard problem.
