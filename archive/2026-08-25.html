Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
AI Agent

A chatbot answers a question. An "agent" is AI given a goal and allowed to take a chain of actions on its own to reach it — searching, clicking, filling forms, calling other software — checking in with a human only sometimes, if at all.

Open-weight model

Most AI companies keep their model's internal "weights" (the numbers that make it work) secret. An open-weight model publishes them, letting anyone download and run the model on their own hardware — which is why a platform like Hugging Face, which hosts thousands of these, matters so much to who gets to build with AI.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Civility norms

The unwritten rules about how "reasonably" you're allowed to argue for a position. Philosophers have long debated whether demanding politeness in debate is a neutral good — or whether it quietly protects whoever already holds power, by making forceful dissent look like bad behavior.

Technocracy

Rule by experts and technical specialists rather than elected representatives. It sounds efficient — decisions made by whoever knows the subject best — but critics argue it can hide political choices behind a veneer of neutral expertise, making them harder to question or vote out.
