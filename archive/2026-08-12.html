Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Compute deal

A multi-year contract where an AI company locks in the right to use a fixed amount of computing power (measured in megawatts of data-center capacity) from a provider, often years before the data center is even finished being built. Think of it like pre-ordering a huge, guaranteed block of electricity and hardware, rather than buying it as you need it.

Vendor financing

When a company that sells you something also helps arrange the loans or investment you use to pay for it — as Nvidia is doing for AI data centers. It can grow an industry fast, but it also means the seller has a stake in convincing everyone that demand will keep growing.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Panpsychism

The idea that consciousness — some form of inner experience — isn't unique to brains, but is a basic feature of matter itself, present in some minimal way even in particles. It doesn't mean rocks think; it means the building blocks of everything might have a spark of "what it's like to be" that combines, in complex systems like brains, into full-blown minds.

Substrate independence

The idea that consciousness depends on how information is organized and processed, not on what physical material does the processing — meaning, in theory, a mind could arise from carbon-based neurons, silicon chips, or an alien chemistry we haven't imagined yet, as long as the pattern is complex enough.
