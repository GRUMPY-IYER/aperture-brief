Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Acqui-hire

When a bigger company buys a smaller one mainly to get its team and technology rather than its revenue or customers — like Amazon buying DuckLabs for the engineers behind DuckDB, a popular free database tool, while promising to keep that tool free for everyone else to use.

Age assurance

Technology that tries to verify how old a user actually is before letting them use an app or service, without collecting so much personal data (like ID scans) that it creates its own privacy risk — the tricky balance regulators are now demanding platforms solve for kids under 18.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Analytic idealism

The view, associated with philosopher Bernard Kastrup, that mind or consciousness is the fundamental fabric of reality, and the physical world we perceive is more like an image or dashboard generated by that underlying mental process — the reverse of the usual assumption that matter comes first and mind emerges from it.

Applied philosophy

Using philosophical training, like careful reasoning about ethics, ambiguity and edge cases, to do practical work outside academia — for instance, helping an AI company decide what values or rules to build into a chatbot, which is why tech firms are increasingly hiring philosophy graduates.
