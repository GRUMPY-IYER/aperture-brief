OpenAI Backs a Bill Requiring Outside Safety Audits of AI Models

OpenAI is publicly supporting a provision of the bipartisan FRONTIER Act that would require leading AI labs to let independent verification organizations inspect their models for safety. It is the first time the company has backed a federal AI safety mandate outright.

Why it matters for you: Worth tracking if you ship AI features: third-party model audits may go from optional trust signal to compliance requirement.

AI Safety Groups Have Quietly Built a Big Capitol Hill Lobbying Operation

Billionaire-backed AI safety organizations like ControlAI have ramped up lobbying in Washington, holding hundreds of meetings with lawmakers. The push comes as Congress weighs how aggressively to regulate frontier AI development ahead of the midterms.

Why it matters for you: The regulatory timeline for AI product decisions may be moving faster in DC than most roadmaps assume.

Techmeme

Apple Folds Apple TV and Arcade Into iCloud+ at No Extra Cost

Every paid iCloud+ tier, even the cheapest 50GB plan, now includes Apple TV and Apple Arcade free in more than 100 countries. The US, UK and most of Europe are notably excluded, with the rollout instead spanning Africa, Asia, the Middle East and Latin America.

Why it matters for you: A clean example of bundling as a retention lever: fold two underused subscriptions into one people already pay for.

A Solar Desalination Device Turns Seawater to Fresh Water Without Toxic Brine

University of Rochester engineers built a solar-powered desalination device using laser-etched 'black metal' that wicks seawater upward, leaving salts and minerals behind as a solid rather than the concentrated brine that usually fouls coastlines. The self-cleaning system could even make it economical to recover lithium from the leftover salt.

The New World screwworm, a parasitic fly whose larvae burrow into living flesh, has reached Texas and New Mexico decades after a mass eradication campaign wiped it out. Researchers are racing to revive that strategy before the pest re-establishes itself in US livestock and wildlife.

Philosophers distinguish indoctrination from education by what it does to a person's capacity to reason: it doesn't just install false beliefs, it disables the tools that would let someone question them. The piece asks what actually rebuilds that capacity once it's gone.

Big Think

The Unsettling Idea That Your Mind Is Just Along for the Ride

Epiphenomenalism holds that consciousness is a byproduct of physical brain processes with no causal power of its own; your thoughts don't move your body, they just come along for the ride. It's one of philosophy's least comforting answers to the mind-body problem, precisely because so much of it appears consistent with the science.

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Epiphenomenalism

The view that mental states like thoughts and feelings are caused by brain activity but have no effect on it in return: consciousness as a passenger, not a driver.
