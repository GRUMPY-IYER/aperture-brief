Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Ghost demand

When developers file multiple speculative requests for grid power to secure a place in the queue, without a confirmed customer or the funding behind them. Texas's freeze exists because so much of its 474-gigawatt data-center queue turned out to be exactly this kind of phantom demand.

Preparedness Framework

OpenAI's internal system for rating how dangerous a new model's capabilities are — in areas like cyber, bio and persuasion — before deciding how tightly to restrict access. Astra is the first OpenAI model rated 'Critical' for cyber capability under this framework.

Big Think

How to wait well, according to neuroscience and psychology

Drawing on research from psychologist Sarah Schnitker's lab, this piece argues patience is a trainable skill rather than a fixed trait, and that treating waiting as a meaningful activity rather than dead time measurably improves wellbeing. It's a modern, empirical echo of the old philosophical case for patience as a virtue.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Instrumental vs. intrinsic value

Whether something matters only for what it gets you (instrumental) or for its own sake (intrinsic). Debates over whether nature deserves protection purely for human benefit, or because it has worth independent of us, turn entirely on which kind of value you think it has.

Patience as a virtue

In Aristotle's ethics, a virtue is a trainable disposition, not an innate trait — you become patient by practicing patience, the same way you become brave by acting bravely. Modern psychology's finding that waiting well can be taught is, in that sense, a very old idea in new language.
