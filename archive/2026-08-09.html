Grid interconnection

Before a power plant (or a giant AI data center) can actually draw or supply electricity, it has to be formally connected to the shared regional grid — a slow regulatory and engineering process that now often takes years. It's why AI companies are increasingly building their own dedicated power plants rather than waiting in the interconnection queue, as in the NextEra-Dominion story above.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Non-duality (Advaita)

The idea, central to Advaita Vedanta, that the apparent separation between "you" and "the world" is not fundamentally real — that underneath the sense of being a separate self, there is one undivided awareness that all experience arises within. It's less a belief to adopt than a claim to investigate directly in your own experience.

Idealism (in Wolfram/ruliad terms, above)

The metaphysical position that mind or consciousness — not physical matter — is the fundamental stuff of reality, and that the physical world we perceive is better understood as something generated within or by consciousness, rather than existing independently of it.
