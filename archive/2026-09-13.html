Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Founder ouster (and reinstatement)

A company's board holds the legal power to remove even a founder-CEO, as Automattic's briefly did to Matt Mullenweg — a reminder that founder control usually rests on board goodwill and bylaws, not just reputation or equity.

Readiness gate

A condition a company sets for itself before shipping or going public — usually safety, security or compliance work — rather than one imposed from outside. OpenAI citing unfinished safety work to delay its IPO is a readiness gate it chose for itself.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
The myth of progress

The assumption that history moves in one direction — toward more justice, more freedom, more moral improvement — the way it plainly moves toward more advanced technology. Philosophers like John Gray argue this conflates two very different kinds of progress, and that only one of them is real.

Legitimacy crisis

When a political system stops being trusted to represent the people it governs, even if its formal procedures — elections, courts, legislatures — keep functioning normally. Aeon's essay treats today's democratic backsliding as this kind of crisis, not a personnel problem.
