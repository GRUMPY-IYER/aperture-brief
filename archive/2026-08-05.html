Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Residential Proxy Network

A system that routes internet traffic through ordinary people's home devices — phones, smart TVs, routers — instead of a data center, making the traffic look like it's coming from a regular household rather than a company. Apps get paid to embed hidden code letting outside customers "borrow" a device's connection anonymously. That's exactly what happened in today's top story: an innocent-looking Pac-Man game secretly turned Samsung TVs into paid tunnels for scraping data and training AI models, without owners realizing their internet was being resold to strangers.
