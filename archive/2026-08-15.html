Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Anti-steering rules

App stores used to ban apps from even mentioning that you could pay for something more cheaply on the web. "Anti-steering" is the practice of blocking that kind of signpost. Regulators have been forcing platforms like Apple to drop these rules — which is why Apple is now testing how much of a fee it can still charge on purchases it no longer directly processes.

Industrial control systems (ICS)

These are the specialized computers that physically run things like water pumps, valves and power grids — distinct from the laptops and servers most "cybersecurity" news is about. ICS equipment is often decades old and wasn't built to be internet-connected, which is exactly why hackers targeting infrastructure go after it.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Idealism (philosophy of mind)

Idealism is the view that mind, not matter, is the fundamental stuff of reality — physical things are what mental processes look like from the outside, rather than the other way around. It's the opposite of materialism, which says mind somehow emerges from matter.

Teleology

Teleology is explaining something by its purpose or end goal, rather than just its mechanical cause — saying a heart exists "in order to" pump blood, not just "because of" the biological processes that built it. Philosophers still argue over whether purpose-talk like this is just a useful shorthand or points to something real.
