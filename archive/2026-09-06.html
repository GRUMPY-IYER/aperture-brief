Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Zero-day vulnerability

A software flaw attackers are already exploiting before the vendor has shipped a fix — 'zero days' of warning. It's why patch speed matters operationally: once one is public and unpatched, every device running that software is a live target.

AI training-data lawsuit (fair use)

When an AI company trains a model on copyrighted text without a license, the copyright owner can sue for infringement — the fight now hitting OpenAI, Microsoft and others. Courts are deciding whether training on copyrighted work counts as 'fair use' or requires a license, and the answer will set the price of training data going forward.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Perspectivism

The idea, associated with Nietzsche and revived in current debates, that there's no 'view from nowhere': every claim to truth is made from a particular standpoint, shaped by history, biology or instruments. It doesn't make truth arbitrary — only means access to it is always situated somewhere.

Phronesis (practical wisdom)

Aristotle's term for the skill of judging well in a specific situation, distinct from knowing a general rule. It's why two people can know the same principle and only one makes the wise call — phronesis is knowing how a rule applies here, now.
