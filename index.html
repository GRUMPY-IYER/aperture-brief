Federal Judge Lets NAACP's Clean Air Act Suit Against xAI's Memphis Data Center Proceed

Judge Debra M. Brown denied xAI's motion to dismiss a Clean Air Act suit over its Colossus data center near Memphis, rejecting the company's argument that its 35-plus gas combustion turbines count as exempt 'mobile sources.' The Hacker News

A Shared ChatGPT Link Could Let Attackers Hijack Victims' Accounts and Read Private Chats

Researchers at Parameter found that opening a shared ChatGPT conversation could run attacker code inside a victim's logged-in session, letting it list and read private chats never shared with the attacker. OpenAI paid a $5,000 bounty and shipped a fix within eight days of the report; no real-world exploitation was found.

Why it matters for you: The exploit chained a sandboxed preview, a third-party library's unchecked message listener, and YouTube's presence on ChatGPT's own content-security-policy allowlist — proof that every 'trusted' domain on your CSP is still part of your attack surface, not a safe exemption from it.

Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Citizen suit

A legal mechanism in laws like the Clean Air Act letting private citizens or groups sue a polluter directly, without waiting for a regulator to act — the provision the NAACP used against xAI, and which xAI tried, and failed, to have declared unconstitutional.

Content Security Policy (CSP)

A browser rule, set by a website, listing which external domains its page is allowed to load scripts from. ChatGPT's CSP trusted YouTube, which is normally harmless — until attackers found a way to use that trust to run their own code inside a victim's account.

Aeon

Does the Demand That Everything Have a Reason Lead Straight to Panpsychism?

Philosopher Sam Kimpton-Nye argues that the principle of sufficient reason, the demand that everything have an explanation, pushes naturally toward panpsychism, the view that consciousness is fundamental to matter rather than something assembled from it. He doubts the universe is ever fully intelligible, but finds panpsychism's attempt at that intelligibility illuminating regardless.

Aeon

Your Gut Feeling That Something Is Off Might Be a Form of Perception

Philosopher Marc Moffett argues that intuitions, the instant sense that a stranger is creepy or an argument has gone wrong, work like perception rather than vague feeling: they present a situation as actually being a certain way, and that presentation alone gives you reason to believe it, no further justification required.

Aeon

Cicero's Idea of Manhood Was Self-Control, Not Domination — Even in Defeat

Historian Catharine Edwards traces how Cicero built his model of Roman manhood around courage, self-control and moderation rather than conquest, an ideal he had to keep performing publicly. After Caesar silenced Rome's political forum, Cicero insisted that bearing exile and grief with dignity was still a manly virtue, redefining strength as endurance.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Principle of sufficient reason

The philosophical claim that everything that exists or happens has a reason or cause — nothing is a brute, unexplainable fact. Today's panpsychism essay uses it to argue that if even consciousness needs an explanation, matter itself may have to be conscious all the way down.

Epistemic intuition

A direct, non-inferential sense that a claim is true or a situation is a certain way, treated by some philosophers as a source of evidence in its own right rather than just a hunch. Today's essay argues it works like perception, not guesswork.
