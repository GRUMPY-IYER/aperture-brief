Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Capex (Capital Expenditure)

Money a company spends on long-term physical stuff — data centers, chips, buildings — instead of day-to-day costs like salaries. When you hear Google or Meta are spending hundreds of billions on "AI capex," that's them buying the servers and chips to run AI models, a bet that pays off over years, not the next quarter.

Regulatory Exemption

Permission from a regulator to operate outside a normal rule, usually because the rule wasn't written with your product in mind. Zoox's robotaxi has no steering wheel, so federal car-safety rules (written assuming a human driver) technically don't fit — NHTSA's exemption is a temporary, capped permission slip while permanent rules catch up.
