Data Centers Become a Political Flashpoint After Democrats' Midterm Sweep

Voters rejected data centers in dozens of local ballot measures, and candidates who ran against them won even where the projects weren't on the ballot — including Texas governor-elect Gina Hinojosa, who unseated Greg Abbott. Forecasters at the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts say the Arctic stratospheric polar vortex is weakening in early November — the earliest such disruption on record, with only three comparable November events since 1958. Rossby waves surging into the stratosphere triggered the collapse, raising the odds of cold outbreaks across the Northern Hemisphere this winter.

Aeon

Why the Line Between Religious Belief and Factual Belief Justifies Separating Church and State

Religious credence is chosen and insulated from evidence, while factual belief is involuntary and drives action everywhere else — philosopher Neil Van Leeuwen and collaborators argue this psychological split, not mere tradition, is why liberal democracies bar state religion while still protecting free worship. Policies built on religious conviction can't be tested through public reasoning the way factual claims can.

Aeon

Why Living With Housemates, Not Family, Finally Punctured One Writer's Self-Deceptions

Sharing a London house with unrelated strangers forced essayist Elizabeth Oldfield to confront self-deceptions that family and solitude let her avoid, drawing on Charles Taylor's "buffered self" and Iris Murdoch's idea of seeing past one's own fantasies. Such communities only last, she argues, when built around a purpose bigger than companionship itself.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Credence vs. belief

Philosophers distinguish 'credence' — a stance you can choose to hold in one context (like a pew) and drop in another — from 'belief', which tracks evidence and follows you everywhere, including into a voting booth. The difference explains why you can respect someone's faith and still insist public policy answer to facts.
