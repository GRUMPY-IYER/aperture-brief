Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Group boycott (antitrust)

When competing businesses coordinate to refuse dealing with a third party — the legal theory DOJ is testing against five rival TV networks that briefly, and jointly, stopped covering White House pool events together.

Frontier model

Industry shorthand for an AI model at or near the current capability ceiling, regardless of maker. Meta calling Muse Wonder 'frontier' is a claim about where it ranks against OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, not just a product name.

A Hidden 'Local pH' Factor Can Make or Break a Chemical Reaction

Seoul National University researchers identified local pH — the acidity right at an electrode's surface — as an overlooked variable that decides whether an electrochemical reaction succeeds, and used that insight to raise a target compound's yield from about 16% to 97%. The process builds compounds for plastics, medicines and adhesives using oxygen and electricity instead of a separate peroxide oxidant.

MIT Astronomers Catch a Star Feeding on a Brown Dwarf, a Meal That Could Last Billions of Years

Astronomers found a low-mass star about 300 light-years away, ZTF J0440+2325, steadily drawing material from a companion brown dwarf it orbits every 86.65 minutes, the first system observed in this kind of slow stellar feeding. Rather than consuming its partner in one burst, the star is expected to keep siphoning material for billions of years.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Orthopraxy

The view that a tradition is defined by its shared practices rather than its shared beliefs — the lens Jonny Thomson uses to argue most people are, religiously speaking, more mixed than they think.

Inference to the best explanation

Reasoning from the available evidence to whichever explanation accounts for it most plausibly — the standard McKenzie-McHarg argues should separate a conspiracy theory worth investigating from one that's pure apocalyptic fantasy.
