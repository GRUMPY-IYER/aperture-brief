Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Third-party AI safety evaluator

An outside group given enough access to an AI company's models and internal practices to independently judge their safety, and the right to publish what they find even if it's unflattering — a check meant to be harder to shrug off than the company grading its own homework.

Per-seat AI spend cap

A budget limit a company sets on how much of an employee's AI usage it will pay for automatically, the same way cloud computing costs get metered — once usage-based AI bills get big enough, companies start treating them like any other line item that needs a ceiling.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Gödel's incompleteness theorems

Kurt Gödel proved that in any mathematical system rich enough to do arithmetic, there will always be true statements that can't be proved within that system — certainty has a ceiling, even in math.

Principle of sufficient reason

The idea, associated with the philosopher Leibniz, that everything which exists has an explanation for why it exists and why it's this way rather than some other way — nothing is ultimately just a brute, reasonless fact.
