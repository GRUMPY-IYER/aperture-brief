Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Opt-out default

A setting that's turned on for everyone automatically, leaving it to each person to notice and switch it off — as opposed to an opt-in default, where nothing happens until you actively choose it. Discord's move to make public messages searchable uses the opt-out version.

Automated license plate reader (ALPR)

Camera networks, like those Flock Safety sells to police departments, that photograph and log every passing vehicle's plate, time and location — building a searchable record of where cars, and the people driving them, have been, not just the ones linked to a crime.

A Technician Cracked a 1,600-Year-Old Roman Glass Mystery

The Lycurgus Cup, a 4th-century Roman glass vessel in the British Museum, turns jade green in reflected light and ruby red when light passes through it, and no one had reproduced the effect until University of Sheffield technician Lisa Hollands developed a recipe believed to rely on tiny gold and silver nanoparticles. A Laser Upgrade Lets Cryo-Electron Microscopes See Proteins They Used to Miss

UC Berkeley researchers added a high-power laser phase plate to a cryo-electron microscope, shifting the electron beam's phase to sharpen contrast enough to resolve proteins as small as 50 kilodaltons — far below what conventional cryo-EM can usually see. The team hopes the technique will eventually reveal how molecular machines work together inside living cells.

Philosophy

Aeon

How Intuitions Actually Work

Philosopher Marc Moffett argues intuitions — from a gut sense a stranger is dangerous to a philosopher's reaction to a thought experiment — function like perception: they present their content as simply actual, and that presentational quality, not some special intellectual feeling, is what lets them justify belief without appealing to further beliefs.

Aeon

Does the Demand That Everything Be Explainable Lead to Panpsychism?

If the principle of sufficient reason — the demand that everything have an intelligible explanation — is true, the essay argues, consciousness may have to be a fundamental feature of matter itself, closing the otherwise unexplained gap between non-conscious stuff and conscious minds. Mørch and Spinoza reach similar conclusions by different routes, though the author doubts the reasoning can fully succeed.

Essentia Foundation

Reason Can't Tell You What to Care About

Reason alone cannot create inherent worth, the philosopher argues — it can only refine the needs and commitments we already have. Value begins instead with felt need, especially recognizing other people's inner lives, and reason's job is to help that coexist with others who value differently, not to settle what's worth caring about.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Presentational intuition

The idea that intuitions — gut senses as well as philosophers' reactions to thought experiments — don't feel like a special kind of thinking so much as a kind of perception: they present something as simply true, the way seeing a chair presents a chair, which is what lets them justify a belief on their own.

Panpsychism

The view that consciousness isn't something that only switches on in brains, but a basic property of matter itself, present in some form everywhere — proposed partly to avoid having to explain how non-conscious stuff could ever give rise to conscious minds in the first place.
