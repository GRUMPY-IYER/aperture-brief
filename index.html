Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Autonomous penetration testing ("pentesting")

A "pen test" is when a security team tries to break into a company's own computer systems on purpose, to find weak spots before criminals do. Traditionally this was done by hired human hackers, once a year, checking only a small slice of the network. "Autonomous" pentesting (like Horizon3's product in today's Top Tech) uses AI agents to do this continuously and automatically, probing the entire network every week instead of once a year — trading a once-a-year snapshot for an always-on stress test.

Series E funding round

Startups raise money in labeled rounds — Seed, then Series A, B, C, and so on — with each later letter generally meaning the company is more mature, proven, and valuable than the last. A "Series E" like Horizon3's $250 million raise means investors have already backed this company through four earlier rounds and are now betting on it at a $2 billion valuation, a sign it has real, growing revenue rather than just a promising idea.
