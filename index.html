Anthropic's New AI Model Can Identify More Software Bugs Than Ever. Microsoft Is Struggling to Fix Them Fast Enough.

Anthropic's Mythos model is flagging security bugs faster than Microsoft can patch them, according to documents reviewed by ProPublica detailing a "mad dash" to close holes before hackers find them first.

Why it matters for you: AI is now good enough to out-pace human patching capacity, not just human coding capacity. If you're scoping AI-assisted tooling, the bottleneck shifts from "can it find problems" to "can your org triage and fix at the new speed" — the same capacity question is showing up below with Apple's bug bounty program.

TECHMEME, CITING NEW YORK TIMES

An Unsecured Police Dashboard Exposed China's Extensive Tracking of Foreigners

A researcher found an unsecured "Dynamic Control Platform for Overseas Personnel" built for a Chinese public security bureau, logging nearly 12,000 entries — including 300+ foreign journalists — by fusing facial recognition, hospital visits, and transit data.

Why it matters for you: This is a case study in what happens when disparate data sources (cameras, payments, transit, medical) get joined into one dashboard without basic access controls. Any product that aggregates user data across systems inherits this exact risk profile — access control isn't a checkbox, it's the whole ballgame.

TECHMEME, CITING FINANCIAL TIMES

Apple Caps Bug Bounty Submissions After a Deluge of AI-Assisted Reports

Apple introduced a cap and 30-day cool-off period on bug bounty submissions after AI-generated reports — often superficially plausible but wrong — flooded its review pipeline; researchers can request higher quotas.

Why it matters for you: "AI lowers the cost of generating plausible-looking output" is a double-edged sword for any submission-based product (support tickets, code review, applications). Apple's fix — rate limits plus an internal AI triager — is becoming the standard playbook worth knowing.

Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Bug Bounty Program

A company pays outside researchers to find and report security flaws before criminals do. It only works if the review team can keep up — which is exactly what broke when AI let anyone generate reports faster than humans could check them.

AI Slop

Low-effort, mass-produced content or reports generated by AI that look legitimate at a glance but are often wrong, fake, or useless on inspection — like the fabricated satellite images and bug reports flooding platforms today.
