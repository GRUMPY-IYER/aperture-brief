Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Supply chain risk designation

A label the US government can slap on a vendor it deems untrustworthy, which can bar federal agencies from buying its products. It's meant for things like hardware with hidden backdoors — using it to punish a company for refusing to change its software's safety rules, as a judge just ruled happened to Anthropic, is what made this case unusual.

Safety guardrails

Rules built into an AI model that stop it from being used certain ways — for example, refusing to help design weapons or conduct mass surveillance — even if the customer asking has the technical ability to override them.

Philosophy 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Analytic idealism

The view, associated with Bernard Kastrup, that consciousness is the fundamental substance of reality — not something that emerges from matter, but the "stuff" everything else, including matter, is made of. Physical objects are what conscious experience looks like from the outside.

The hard problem of consciousness

Even if we map every neuron firing in the brain, why does any of it feel like something from the inside? Explaining the mechanics is the "easy" part; explaining why there's subjective experience at all is the "hard problem."
