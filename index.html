Tech 101 for newbies

Today's Concept, Explained Simply
Agentic AI

Instead of answering one question at a time like a chatbot, an "agentic" AI model is given a broad goal and then plans its own steps, uses tools, checks its own work, and keeps iterating — sometimes for hours or days — before returning a finished result. Astra's math proofs are an early example: nobody fed it the answer, it worked the problem.

Formal proof checker (Lean)

Lean is software that verifies a mathematical proof is logically airtight, line by line. When OpenAI says Astra "proved" something, it published machine-checkable Lean certificates alongside the claim — so outside mathematicians can verify the logic themselves instead of taking the company's word for it.
